## UniversalSync 2 is coming

UniversalSync version 1.x will soon be replaced by UniversalSync version 2.x.
Version 2 brings a new database format and various cleanups, but remains
protocol compatible with UniversalSync 1.

More detailed information about UniversalSync 2 can be found in the release
notes at https://github.com/yashhasan570/UniversalSync/releases.

This release is also available as:

* APT repository: https://apt.universalsync.net/

* Docker image: `docker.io/universalsync/universalsync:{{.version}}` or `ghcr.io/universalsync/universalsync:{{.version}}`
  (`{docker,ghcr}.io/universalsync/universalsync:1` to follow just the major version)
